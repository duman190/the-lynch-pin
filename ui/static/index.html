    How to read the Lynch Pin
    
      
    
        
    
          
    PEG = P/E ÷ Growth. Forward P/E divided by the multi-source 5Y EPS growth estimate. The bell curve is the stock's own reconstructed 5-year forward-PEG history; the red marker is today.
    
          
    Dev (SD) — how many standard deviations today's PEG sits from its historical mean. Negative = cheaper than usual.
    
          
    Bull / Base / Bear ROI — annualised 5-year return under a terminal-multiple framework with growth decay.
    
          
    Quant scans, not financial advice. Math can be mistaken. Investing involves risk. Always DYOR. 🫶
